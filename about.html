About

Sophia Pasalis is a filmmaker and writer based in the UK. She recently completed her MPhil in Film and Screen by Advanced Study with Distinction at the University of Cambridge. She is a graduate of Harvard University, where she studied History and Literature and Art, Film, and Visual Studies. Along with producing her own projects, she has supported other filmmakers, musicians, artists, and organizations with cinematography, creative direction, and editing.
